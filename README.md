New Step

Take Your First Step Towards Your Future.

New Step is an online guidance tool for Thanaweya Amma, IGCSE, and IB students in Egypt that want to find out how to pursue higher education after high school. It combines information about universities, scholarships, student accommodation, and a budget calculator into one tool for the student to use, rather than him/her having to go back and forth between ten Facebook groups, PDFs, and outdated forum threads.

Why I created this

It's incredibly difficult to choose a university in Egypt. The coordination (تنسيق) system changes every year, the deadlines for the scholarships are dispersed over dozens of different sites in different languages, and no one tells you how much money it would cost to live away from home during your studies. I thought that there should be a single website where the student can compare all of that easily, without any help from older siblings or Facebook groups.
